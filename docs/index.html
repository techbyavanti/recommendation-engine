Six strategies and their differences

All strategies get the same baseline candidates. search-service supplies a large pool with hybrid retrieval (dense and lexical). Then a hard eligibility filter removes the products that are not eligible. This occurs before a strategy starts. Each strategy then does one of these tasks: it keeps the candidates as they are, it changes their order, or it adds or removes products. No strategy does its own retrieval. The numbers below come from the independent WANDS relevance evaluation in RESULTS.md. This evaluation uses the human relevance grades in WANDS. No strategy uses these grades for training or tuning. The strategies use labels from clickstream data. (RESULTS.md has the full method, a second table for implicit clickstream data, and the limits of the results.)
